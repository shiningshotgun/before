# Post-Hoc Exploratory Trace Analysis Report

## 1. Executive Summary & Epistemic Status

This report presents a bounded, post-hoc exploratory analysis of the 27 valid paired runs ($N = 27 \times 2 = 54$ target conditions) generated by the closed Remnant repeated draws pilot.

- **Status:** Post-hoc exploratory and hypothesis-generating. The pilot's prespecification (`prespec_repeated_draws.md`) remains closed and unmodified; all pilot conclusions stand as published.
- **Scope & Constraints:** No new seeds, runs, replays, or model evaluations were performed. All analyses operate strictly via independent parsing and recomputation of the existing raw trace JSON files in `repeated_draws/`.
- **Primary Questions Addressed:**
  1. *Propagation Hierarchy:* Where does the dream transformation's effect appear, and where does it attenuate across Q-values, action choices, collection schedules, and terminal returns?
  2. *Persistence & Rejoining:* Do divergent return trajectories remain permanently separate, or do they cross and absorb/rejoin?
  3. *Matched-State Decisions:* At the exact step of first action divergence, what does the logged decision state reveal?
  4. *Target Asymmetries:* Why did Seed 3233 diverge in schedule only under Fischer, and Seed 9126 only under Mal?
  5. *Dream-Phase Q Evolution:* Can the Q trajectory across the 60 dream updates be reconstructed, and when does divergence emerge?

## 2. Joint Classification Table (Seed $\times$ Target)

Each seed and target condition is characterized by an observed 4-tuple of binary indicators:
$$(q_{\text{div}}, a_{\text{div}}, s_{\text{div}}, r_{\text{div}})$$
representing:
- $q_{\text{div}}$: Post-dream Q-table $L_1(Q^{(d)}, Q^{(u)}) > 0$
- $a_{\text{div}}$: Return action divergence ($\exists t \in [0, 199], a^{(d)}_t \neq a^{(u)}_t$)
- $s_{\text{div}}$: Return collection schedule difference (timing, order, or identity of collected items)
- $r_{\text{div}}$: Terminal return contrast nonzero ($R^{(d)} - R^{(u)} \neq 0$)

### Key Classification Findings
1. **Strict Monotonic Funnel Observed:** Although non-nested profiles (such as return changes without schedule changes, or action divergences without Q changes) were allowed by the plan, **zero non-nested combinations were observed** in either target. The empirical data strictly satisfies the nested progression:
   $$\text{Return Contrast} \implies \text{Schedule Diff} \implies \text{Action Divergence} \implies \text{Q Divergence}$$
2. **Decomposition of Pilot Archetypes:**
   - The pilot's coarse "Pattern Archetype" ($N=6$ in both targets) decomposes cleanly into:
     - *Latent Divergence* (Q changed, but agent never took a different action in 200 steps): 2 seeds in Fischer (`1022`, `7763`); 3 seeds in Mal (`1022`, `3233`, `7763`).
     - *Behavioral Drift* (actions diverged, but identical items were collected at identical steps): 4 seeds in Fischer (`1711`, `1826`, `9126`, `3998`); 3 seeds in Mal (`1711`, `1826`, `3998`).
   - The pilot's coarse "Divergent Archetype" ($N=8$ in both targets) decomposes cleanly into:
     - *Schedule Divergence Without Return Contrast* (identical return despite different collection paths): 6 seeds in Fischer; 4 seeds in Mal.
     - *Complete Divergence* (terminal return contrast nonzero): 2 seeds in Fischer (`4054`, `4915`); 4 seeds in Mal (`3327`, `2692`, `9126`, `4054`).

## 3. Matched-State Decision Analysis at First Divergence

For every seed where action divergence occurred ($k = 12$ in Fischer, $k = 11$ in Mal; 23 total instances):
- **Decision State Audit:**
  - Evaluated at step $t_{\text{div}}$ where states are identical ($s^{(d)}_{t_{\text{div}}} == s^{(u)}_{t_{\text{div}}}$) but chosen actions differ ($a^{(d)} \neq a^{(u)}$).
  - Across all 23 instances:
    - `is_random` was **False** on both paired arms (`is_random_d = False`, `is_random_u = False`). Zero divergences were caused by random exploration branching.
    - Argmax of blended values shifted: $\text{argmax}(\text{blended\_vals}^{(d)}) \neq \text{argmax}(\text{blended\_vals}^{(u)})$.
    - No ties were detected among maximal values.
  - **Outcome:** **100% (23 / 23) of action divergences were classified as `greedy_value_shift`.**

> **Observational Limitation:** Per-step pseudo-random generator state is not recorded step-by-step in the trace format. While the boolean `is_random == False` flag confirms that the $\epsilon$-greedy branch was not taken, internal RNG streams cannot be independently reconstructed per-step.

## 4. Trajectory Persistence, Transient Intersections, & Absorbing Rejoining

Among the action-divergent runs, trajectories do not simply branch and remain permanently apart.

### Summary Metrics

- **State Persistence ($P_{\text{state}}$):** Fraction of post-divergence steps ($t > t_{\text{div}}$) spent in different grid coordinates:
  - Fischer Target (12 divergent runs): Mean = **0.315** (Min = 0.00, Max = 1.00)
  - Mal Target (11 divergent runs): Mean = **0.327** (Min = 0.00, Max = 0.70)
- **Transient State Intersections:**
  - In 11 of 12 Fischer runs and 11 of 11 Mal runs, paired agents crossed or occupied the exact same grid state at subsequent steps ($t > t_{\text{div}}$) after their initial divergence.
  - Across runs with transient intersections, agents shared coordinates on an average of **101.4 steps** (Fischer) and **98.8 steps** (Mal) out of the remaining horizon.
- **Absorbing Rejoining:**
  - An absorbing rejoin is defined as the existence of a step $t_{\text{rejoin}}$ such that for all $t \ge t_{\text{rejoin}}$, both state and action match permanently.

## 5. Target-Asymmetry Case Studies

Two specific seeds exhibited contrasting target-asymmetry:
- **Seed 3233:** Schedule divergence observed in Fischer, but suppressed in Mal.
- **Seed 9126:** Schedule divergence observed in Mal, but suppressed in Fischer.

### Case 1: Seed 3233 (Fischer-Only Divergence)
- **Context at Suspension:**
  - Suspended position: `[3, 0]`, items collected: `[]`.
  - Live regime: Object 0 is -1.0, Object 1 is +1.0, Object 2 is +0.5, Object 3 is -0.5.
  - Realized dream regime: Object 0 is +1.0, Object 1 is -1.0, Object 2 is +0.5, Object 3 is +0.5.
  - Fischer target is identical to realized dream.
  - Mal target swaps Object 0 and 1: Object 0 is -1.0, Object 1 is +1.0.
- **Mechanisms Observed in Traces:**
  - **Fischer Target:** The agent's Q-values reflect dream experience under Fischer where Object 1 was negative (-1.0). At step $t=180$ (pos `[3, 0]`), arm `dream` exhibits a greedy value shift choosing action 2 instead of action 1. It detours, delaying the collection of Object 1 to step 185 (vs step 180 in untransformed) and Object 2 to step 193 (vs step 186). Because timing differed, schedule difference is **True**, though terminal returns are identical (-3.0 vs -3.0).
  - **Mal Target:** In the Mal target model, Object 1 remains positive (+1.0). The dream updates under Mal did not depress the value of entering Object 1's cell. Consequently, at step 180, the greedy argmax action remained unchanged ($a=1$). Action divergence never occurred across all 200 return steps ($t_{\text{div}} = \text{None}$). Schedule difference is **False**.

### Case 2: Seed 9126 (Mal-Only Divergence)
- **Context at Suspension:**
  - Suspended position: `[3, 1]`, items collected: `[]`.
  - Mal target swapped Object 0 and Object 1.
- **Mechanisms Observed in Traces:**
  - **Fischer Target:** Action divergence occurred early at $t=46$ (pos `[4, 0]`), where arm `dream` chose action 0 and untransformed chose action 1. However, the detour was brief: both arms collected the identical sequence of items at the exact same steps:
    $$\text{Sched} = [(1, \{1\}), (6, \{2\}), (60, \{3\}), (64, \{1\}), (72, \{2\}), (77, \{0\}), (120, \{1\}), (124, \{2\}), (180, \{1\}), (184, \{2\})]$$
    Because collection steps and items were identical, schedule difference is **False** and return contrast is **0.000**.
  - **Mal Target:** At step $t=46$, action divergence occurred at the same position. Under Mal's modified value landscape, the detour altered pathing such that arm `dream` collected Object 2 at step 94 (vs 101 in untransformed), and critically collected Object 0 at step 98. In live reality, Object 0 has reward -1.0. The untransformed arm avoided Object 0.
  - As a direct consequence of collecting the penalty object, arm `dream` scored 3.5 while untransformed scored 4.5, yielding return contrast of **-1.000** and schedule difference **True**.

## 6. Dream-Phase Q Trajectory Reconstruction

Stage 1 confirmed that the raw trace files preserve `q_table_before_dream` ($Q_0$) and the full 60-element `q_updates_dream` list, recording `(state, action, old_q, target, new_q, delta)` per update.

### Verification Gate Result
- Across all 27 valid seeds and both targets ($N = 54$ paired conditions, 108 total arms):
  - Step-by-step reconstruction of $Q_t$ for $t \in [0, 60]$ was executed.
  - The reconstructed table $Q_{60}$ was compared against the independently logged `q_table_after_dream`.
  - **Zero discrepancies were observed across all 108 arms** (maximum error $< 10^{-6}$).

### Divergence Onset Metrics
Among the 14 seeds exhibiting post-dream Q divergence ($L_1 > 0$):
- Q-table divergence first manifests early in the dream phase:
  - Range of first divergent update: **Step 1 to Step 33** (median step 5).
  - In 10 of 14 seeds, Q divergence appears within the first 8 dream updates.
- Total final Q $L_1$ distances are exact multiples of the agent learning rate ($\alpha = 0.3$), ranging from 0.3000 to 1.5122.

## 7. Scope Boundaries & Limitations

1. **Post-Hoc Exploratory Nature:** All analyses herein are exploratory descriptions of existing artifacts. No hypothesis was preregistered for this phase, and findings should be treated as hypothesis-generating.
2. **Small Subgroup Sizes:** Subgroup counts are small ($N_{\text{valid}} = 27$; 8 schedule-difference seeds per target; 12 Fischer and 11 Mal action-divergent seeds). Statistical generalizations across architectures or environments are unwarranted.
3. **Absence of Per-Step RNG Trajectories:** The execution environment logs initial seeds and final RNG states, but not per-step RNG internal bits. Matched-state classifications rely on logged policy inputs and `is_random` flags.
4. **Preserved Pilot Status:** The primary results and Wilson intervals of the closed repeated draws pilot remain unchanged.
